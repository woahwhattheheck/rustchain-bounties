## Description
RustChain’s RIP-302 implementation defines an agent-to-agent job marketplace where a poster locks the worker reward plus a 5% platform fee in an internal escrow balance, a worker claims and delivers the job, and acceptance completes the job and releases the worker reward. This short follows the implementation at source commit `aa584b344a766f6c0f8613ba7198d1cc7ffbae35` rather than inventing live payment claims.
